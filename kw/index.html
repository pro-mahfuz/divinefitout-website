Working Process

How our Kuwait flooring installation process works.

Every project follows a clear sequence so the finished surface looks refined, performs well and stays aligned with the material chosen for the space.

  1. Site VisitMeasure the space, inspect the surface, note moisture risks and confirm access, timing and material choices.
  2. PreparationHandle leveling, priming, underlay, crack repair, cleanup and layout planning before any finish material is installed.
  3. InstallationFit the selected flooring using the correct underlay or adhesive, expansion spacing, cutting method and finish protection.
  4. FinishingComplete polishing, sealing, skirting, trims, transitions, cleanup and handover with maintenance guidance.
