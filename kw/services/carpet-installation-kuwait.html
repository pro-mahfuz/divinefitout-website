Working Process

How our carpet installation process works

Soft flooring performs best with a structured workflow, from accurate measurements and base preparation through to trimming and final checks.

01

Review and measure

We assess the room size, shape, access and whether the project involves replacement or a new installation.

02

Prepare the base

The floor is checked for smoothness and readiness, with leveling or underlay preparation handled where required.

03

Fit and align

The carpet is installed with attention to seams, pattern direction, edge trimming and threshold transitions.

04

Finish and hand over

Edge work, cleanup and final inspection complete the project before the space is handed back.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet installation in Kuwait

Do you install both residential carpet and office carpet in Kuwait?

Yes. We install carpet for bedrooms, family rooms, offices, meeting rooms, hospitality spaces, corridors and other selected commercial interiors across Kuwait.

Can you install wall-to-wall carpet and carpet tiles?

Yes. Our carpet installation service covers wall-to-wall carpet, office carpet tiles and other soft-flooring formats depending on the layout and use of the space.

Do you provide underlay fitting and old carpet replacement?

Yes. We can help with underlay fitting, removal of old carpet, replacement projects, trimming, seam handling and finishing details where needed.

Yes. Carpet is widely used in Kuwait homes, offices, hotels and selected retail interiors where clients want comfort, quieter spaces and a softer finished look.
